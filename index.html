Beyond Consumer Choices: The Matthew 25 Act

Purchasing power creates immediate market signals for ethical brands, but voluntary consumer boycotts cannot dismantle subsidized global cartels alone. The Matthew 25 Act establishes sliding-scale social tariffs, closes Tariff Act Section 307 customs enforcement loopholes, and mandates that foreign manufacturers prove living-wage supply chains free of forced labor before landing at U.S. ports of entry.
